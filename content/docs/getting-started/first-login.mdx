You are signed in as the admin you made on `/setup`. Now choose how everyone else signs in. All of it is in **Settings** (the gear in the admin menu); changes apply at once, no restart.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Turn on Steam sign-in

CS2 players sign in with Steam. Open **Settings** > **Sign-in providers** > **Steam**, paste a **Steam Web API key** and switch Steam on.

Get the key at [steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey](https://steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey). Any domain name works in Steam's form. The key is stored encrypted with `SESSION_SECRET`.

Discord, Google, GitHub, Twitch and OpenID Connect are on the same page, if you want them as well. A player can link several of them to one account on their **Connections** page. See [Sign-in providers](/guides/sign-in).

</Step>

<Step>

### Make the other admins

Two ways:

- **Username accounts.** **Settings** > **Sign-in providers** > **Accounts**: enter a username and a starting password, and switch **Admin** on. At their first sign-in (on `/login/admin`) they choose their own password, add an email if the site sends email, and can turn on two-step verification.
- **Steam players.** Let them sign in with Steam once, then open **Players**, edit the player and switch **Admin** on. Or list their Steam IDs (or emails) under **Settings** > **Sign-in providers** > **Admin access**, and they are admins from their next sign-in.

</Step>

<Step>

### Set up email (optional)

Without email everything still works: admins set new passwords for username accounts by hand. With it, people can reset a forgotten password themselves, and players can ask for an email when a tournament opens its sign-up.

Open **Settings** > **Email**, fill in your SMTP server, the sender address and the site address, save, switch **Send email** on and send a test email.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Players, teams and bans

- A player's account is created the first time they sign in. They pick their games, make a team or join one in **Teams**, and sign it up for tournaments themselves. You can also make teams and players by hand on **Teams** and **Players**.
- **Players** > a player's menu > **Ban** locks a player out: they can't sign in, and their public page shows only their name. **Delete** removes everything about them; their old matches stay, shown as "Deleted player".
