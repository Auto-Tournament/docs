This page runs a CS2 tournament. For CS2 you need at least one server that shows as online on the **Servers** page: see [Connect a CS2 server](/getting-started/connect-server). Games without servers work the same way, but players report the score themselves: see [Manual reporting](/guides/manual-reporting).

<Callout type="warn">
  Auto Tournament runs one tournament at a time. When you create a new tournament, the old one and its matches are deleted. Player rating history is kept.
</Callout>

<Steps>

<Step>

### Create teams

1. Open **Teams**.
2. Click **Create Team**, or **Import JSON** to add many teams at once.
3. Give each team a name, a tag and its players.

For CS2, every player on a roster needs a Steam account: the server lets players in by Steam ID. A player who signed in with another provider links Steam on their connections page. See [Teams and players](/guides/teams-and-players) for the import format.

</Step>

<Step>

### Set up the tournament

Open **Tournament setup** and go through the steps:

1. **Game**: Counter-Strike 2.
2. **Basics**: the name.
3. **Format**: the tournament type (for example single elimination, see [Tournament formats](/guides/tournament-formats)) and the match format, best of 1, 3 or 5.
4. **Teams and sign-up**: pick the teams. Single and double elimination need 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 or 128 teams.
5. **Maps and veto**: the map pool, round rules and overtime. Map veto needs a pool of exactly 7 maps; **Active Duty** works if you are not sure.
6. **Event page**: what the public tournament page shows.
7. **Review**: check everything and click **Create Tournament**.

The bracket is generated. Check it on the **Bracket** page. You can change settings and generate again until you start.

</Step>

<Step>

### Tell the players where to go

Players sign in at your Auto Tournament address, with any sign-in method you turned on. They land on their own player page: it shows their next match, the map veto when it is their turn, and the button to join the server.

Each team also has a page at `/team/<team-id>`. On **Teams**, click the link icon next to a team (**Copy team match link**) to copy it. Send players the [guide for players](/guides/players).

</Step>

<Step>

### Start

Click **Start Tournament**. For each match that can be played:

1. The two teams do the map veto on their player pages.
2. Auto Tournament picks a free server and loads the match.
3. Players join the server and type `.ready` in chat.
4. The match goes live. You see the score on the **Matches** page.
5. When the match ends, the winner moves on in the bracket and the server is free for the next match.

If there are more matches than servers, the extra matches wait until a server is free. With [auto-scaling](/guides/host-cs2-tournament) on, the platform starts or creates servers for them.

</Step>

</Steps>
