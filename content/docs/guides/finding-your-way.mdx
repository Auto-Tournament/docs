Each thing in Auto Tournament has one page, and the other pages link to it.

## The top bar

| Item | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| The logo | **Home**: your next match, live tournaments and your teams. |
| **Play** | Matchmaking: the queue, your party and the lobby. Only when matchmaking is on. |
| **Tournaments** | Every tournament on the site (`/tournaments`). |
| **Leaderboards** | Ratings per game. With matchmaking on, a **Matchmaking** mode shows its ladder. |
| **Community** | **Teams** and **Players** (`/community/teams`, `/community/players`), and a game's own pages such as **Highlights**. |
| **Manage** | Admins only: the admin dashboard. |

When you have a match, a button in the top bar says where it stands (for example "Your turn in veto") and opens its match room.

## The match room

Every match has one page, `/match/<slug>`. Everyone sees the result, the maps and what the game recorded. A player in the match also gets their part while it is on: the map veto, the server with **Connect to Server**, and the live score. For games without servers, it has the form to report the result.

Home, your profile, your team's page and the tournament's Overview show your next match with an **Open match room** button.

## Teams and players

- A team's page is `/team/<team-id>`: the roster, stats, history and its next match. Captains and owners manage the roster at `/team/<team-id>/manage`.
- A player's page is `/player/<id>`.

## Manage

**Manage** is the admin dashboard, with its own header in place of the top bar. **Back to site** returns to the public pages. The menu on the left is the whole admin menu:

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | **Needs you**: what to decide now, and the site at a glance. |
| **Tournaments** | The tournaments being run or set up, then **All tournaments**. |
| **History** | Every played match (with **Import**), and highlights. |
| **People** | Teams, players and reports. |
| **Setup** | Templates and ratings, modules, settings, license, system. |

A game adds its own pages, for example CS2's **Servers** and **Maps**.

One tournament has its own address in Manage, `/manage/tournaments/<id>/…`, with tabs: **Matches**, **Bracket**, **Disputes** (games with reported results), **Setup** and its public page. A link to one of them always opens that tournament.

## Old addresses

Old links keep working: `/browse`, `/browse/teams`, `/browse/players`, `/t/team/<id>`, `/play/leaderboard`, `/tournament/<id>/match`, `/manage/tournament`, `/manage/bracket`, `/manage/matches` and `/manage/disputes` all send you to their new place.
