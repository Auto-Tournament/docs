Players click **Find match**, wait in a queue, accept, and play a CS2 match on a free server. Nobody has to set anything up per match. Matchmaking is experimental: it is off by default and only admins can use it until you open it to players.

<Callout type="warn">
  Matchmaking is new in 3.1 betas. Try it with friends before you open it to everyone.
</Callout>

## What you need

- The CS2 module, with the **Active Duty** map pool (Maps). Matches are played on a random map from it.
- At least one [Ready Up](/cs2/ready-up) server the platform can start, for example on a machine linked with [CS2 Server Manager](/cs2/server-manager). Tournament matches always get a free server first.
- Ten players searching at the same time.

## Turn it on

<Steps>

<Step>

### Switch the feature on

**Settings → Experimental → Matchmaking**. Admins now see **Play** in the top bar.

</Step>

<Step>

### Open it to players

Under the same switch, turn on **Open to players**. Every signed-in player now sees **Play**. Turn on **Leaderboard visible to everyone** if visitors who are not signed in should see the leaderboard.

</Step>

</Steps>

## How a match is found

1. A player picks **Find match**, alone or as a party. A party leader shares the invite link; up to 5 players, always on the same team.
2. A bar at the bottom of every page shows the search and how long it has run.
3. When ten players fit together, everyone gets **Match found** with a sound and has 20 seconds to accept.
4. When all ten accept, the match is created and gets a server. The match room shows both teams, the map and how to join.

Players are matched with others near their rating. The allowed gap starts at ±100 and grows by 50 every 30 seconds, up to 400. After 5 minutes anyone is matched, so a small community still gets games. Teams are split so the total rating is as even as possible.

## Ratings, XP and levels

- **Rating**: each player has a matchmaking rating, separate from tournament ratings. It starts from their tournament rating and settles after a few matches.
- **XP**: every finished match gives 100 XP, +50 for a win (+25 for a draw), up to +50 for performance, and +100 for the first win of the day. XP never goes down.
- **Levels** show how experienced a player is on the platform. Each level needs a bit more XP than the last.
- **Leaderboard** (**Play → Leaderboard**): players with 10 or more matches in the last 30 days.

After a match, the match room shows the score, both scoreboards, the rating change and the XP gained. Players can give each other a thumbs up or a thumbs down (with a reason) for 24 hours. Profiles show the totals, never who voted.

## Leaving and declining

| What happened | Cooldown |
| --- | --- |
| Declined or missed a found match (1st, 2nd, 3rd, then more in 24 h) | 2 min, 10 min, 1 h, 24 h |
| Never joined the server, or left for 5 minutes during a match | 1 h, doubling within 7 days, at most 7 days |

A player who left takes their team's loss in full, gets no XP, and their teammates lose less. Admins can clear a cooldown.
