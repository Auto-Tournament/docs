Players click **Find match**, wait in a queue, accept, and play a CS2 match on a free server: 5v5, 2v2 (Wingman) or 1v1. Nobody has to set anything up per match. It's on from the start, for every signed-in player.

## What you need

- The CS2 module, with the **Active Duty** map pool (Maps). Matches are played on a random map from it.
- At least one [Ready Up](/cs2/ready-up) server the platform can start, for example on a machine linked with [CS2 Server Manager](/cs2/server-manager). Tournament matches always get a free server first.
- Enough players searching at the same time: ten for 5v5, four for 2v2, two for 1v1.

## Who can play

**Settings → Matchmaking**. **Open to players** is on by default: every signed-in player sees **Play**. Turn it off and only admins can find matches, which is handy while you try it with friends. Turn on **Leaderboard visible to everyone** if visitors who aren't signed in should see the leaderboard. The search window below is set there too.

## How a match is found

1. A player picks **Find match**, alone or as a party. A party leader shares the invite link; up to a full team, always on the same side.
2. A bar at the bottom of every page shows the search and how long it has run.
3. When the players for a full match fit together, everyone gets **Match found** with a sound and has 20 seconds to accept.
4. When everyone accepts, the match is created and gets a server. The match room shows both teams, the map and how to join.

Players are matched with others near their rating. By default the allowed gap starts at ±100 and grows by 50 every 30 seconds, up to 400. After 5 minutes anyone is matched, so a small community still gets games. Teams are split so the total rating is as even as possible.

## Ratings, XP and levels

- **Rating**: each player has a matchmaking rating, separate from tournament ratings. It starts from their tournament rating and settles after a few matches.
- **XP**: every finished match gives 100 XP, +50 for a win (+25 for a draw), up to +50 for performance, and +100 for the first win of the day. XP never goes down.
- **Levels** show how experienced a player is on the platform. Each level needs a bit more XP than the last.
- **Leaderboard** (**Play → Leaderboard**): players with 10 or more matches in the last 30 days.

After a match, the match room shows the score, both scoreboards, the rating change and the XP gained. Players can give each other a thumbs up or a thumbs down (with a reason) for 24 hours. Nobody sees who voted.

On a profile, everyone sees the positive types and, from 20 votes, the share of positive votes. Negative types show only to the player and admins.

Votes also nudge matching a little. When there's a choice, players are matched with others of a similar record first. It never stops a match, a party counts as its lowest member, and players with under 20 votes aren't affected. The score itself is never shown.

## Leaving and declining

| What happened | Cooldown |
| --- | --- |
| Declined or missed a found match (1st, 2nd, 3rd, then more in 24 h) | 2 min, 10 min, 1 h, 24 h |
| Never joined the server, or left for 5 minutes during a match | 1 h, doubling within 7 days, at most 7 days |

A player who left takes their team's loss in full, gets no XP, and their teammates lose less. Admins can clear a cooldown.
