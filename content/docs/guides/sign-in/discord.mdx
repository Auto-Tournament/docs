Users sign in with their Discord account. Auto Tournament asks Discord for the `identify` and `email` scopes. See [Sign-in providers](/guides/sign-in) for how accounts and admin access work.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Create a Discord application

1. Open the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications) and sign in.
2. Click **New Application**.
3. Enter a name, for example `Auto Tournament`. Users see this name when they sign in.
4. Tick the box to agree to the Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy, then click **Create**.
5. In the left menu, click **OAuth2**.
6. Under **Client information**, copy the **Client ID**.
7. Click **Reset Secret**, confirm, and copy the **Client Secret**. Discord shows it only once.

</Step>

<Step>

### Add the redirect URL

On the same **OAuth2** page, under **Redirects**, click **Add Redirect** and enter:

```text
FRONTEND_BASE_URL/api/auth/discord/callback
```

For example `https://tournament.example.com/api/auth/discord/callback`. Click **Save Changes**.

</Step>

<Step>

### Turn it on in Auto Tournament

In **Settings → Sign-in → Discord**, paste the Client ID and Client Secret, turn it on and save. The callback URL to register is shown there with a copy button. The **Sign in with Discord** button appears on the login page right away.

Prefer environment variables? Set `AUTH_DISCORD_ENABLED=true`, `DISCORD_CLIENT_ID` and `DISCORD_CLIENT_SECRET` and restart. They are imported once.

</Step>

<Step>

### Test the button

1. Open `FRONTEND_BASE_URL/login` in a private browser window.
2. Click **Sign in with Discord** and approve the request in Discord.
3. You are signed in. Someone new gets an account of their own when registration is open (**Settings → Players & Access**). Steam can be linked later on **/me/connections**.

If Discord shows "Invalid OAuth2 redirect_uri", the redirect URL in step 2 does not match `FRONTEND_BASE_URL` exactly.

</Step>

</Steps>
