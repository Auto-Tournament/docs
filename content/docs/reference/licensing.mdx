Auto Tournament is licensed under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://github.com/Auto-Tournament/auto-tournament/blob/main/LICENSE). The source is public and free to use, change and share for non-commercial purposes. It is not "open source" in the OSI sense.

The rule is simple: **if you earn money from it, you pay full price.** If nobody earns money from it, it's free.

## Free, no permission needed

- Personal use, hobby projects, learning and research, under the license's personal-use terms.
- Tournaments and LAN events where nobody earns money from it: all entry fees and sponsor money go back into the event, and no organizer, volunteer or helper is paid or takes profit.
- Use by the organizations the license lists: charities, schools and universities, public research, public safety or health and environmental protection organizations, and government bodies. They are free even when they charge entry.
- Changing the code, forking it and sharing your changes, as long as you keep the LICENSE and the `Required Notice` line, and your use stays non-commercial.

Running a zero-profit LAN? It's free, no registration needed. Want it in writing? [Tell us about your event](mailto:sivert@autotournament.gg?subject=Free%20LAN%20confirmation%3A%20%3Cevent%3E) (name, dates, website, organizer, roughly how many servers, how entry fees and sponsor money are used, and whether anyone is paid) and we'll send you a free confirmation. It's optional: the license already allows non-commercial use.

## Commercial use needs a license

Examples of commercial use:

- Hosting or running Auto Tournament for someone else for money, including managed hosting and "tournaments as a service".
- Selling Auto Tournament, a modified version of it, or a product or service built on it.
- Running tournaments or events where the organizer makes a profit.
- Using it inside a business, for example an esports organization, venue or company running events.
- Being paid to set up, install or operate Auto Tournament, CS2 Server Manager or Ready Up for someone else, even for a flat fee, and even when that event is itself free.

Forks and modified versions stay under the same license; they can't be relicensed (for example as MIT) or used commercially without a license.

Not sure whether your use counts as commercial? Ask first.

Paid operators must name the event or client they work for when buying. The organizations listed above are free even when they charge entry. Everyone who earns money from it pays the full price, including a paid operator or contractor at an event that is itself free.

## Unlicensed commercial use

If we notify you in writing that your use is commercial and unlicensed, PolyForm gives you 32 days (first notice only) to come into compliance: stop the commercial use or buy a license, and put right past use. To settle past use, we offer a back-dated license at the normal price plus 50%. If you don't come into compliance within 32 days, all your PolyForm licenses end and we may claim compensation under the Norwegian Copyright Act (åndsverkloven § 81).

## Which license covers what

- **PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0:** the platform, 3.0 and later ([auto-tournament](https://github.com/Auto-Tournament/auto-tournament)), the [CS2 Server Manager](https://github.com/Auto-Tournament/cs2-server-manager), the [Ready Up plugin](https://github.com/Auto-Tournament/ready-up), the [game packs](https://github.com/Auto-Tournament/packs), this documentation and the website. Game packs published before 24 September 2026 were MIT and stay MIT; packs published from that date are under PolyForm Noncommercial.
- **MIT:** the CS2 plugin [Auto Tournament CS2](https://github.com/Auto-Tournament/cs2-plugin) (formerly MatchZy Enhanced), free for any use, including paid work. It is forked from [MatchZy](https://github.com/shobhit-pathak/MatchZy), which is MIT, and built on CounterStrikeSharp: GPLv3, with an exception letting plugins use MIT; a noncommercial license isn't allowed. Ready Up is a separate, native plugin, not a fork of MatchZy, and it is not MIT.

## Older versions
