The rule is simple: **if you earn money from it, you need a license.** If nobody earns money from your events, it's free.

## Free, no permission needed

- Personal use, hobby projects, learning and research, under the license's personal-use terms.
- Tournaments and LAN events where nobody earns money from your events: all entry fees and sponsor money go back into the event, and no organizer, volunteer or helper is paid or takes profit.
- Use by the organizations the license lists: charities, schools and universities, public research, public safety or health and environmental protection organizations, and government bodies. They are free even when they charge entry.
- Changing the code, forking it and sharing your changes, as long as you keep the LICENSE and the `Required Notice` line, and your use stays non-commercial.

Running a zero-profit LAN? It's free, no registration needed. Want your install to show up in your account? Get an optional [free key](/guides/license#a-free-key) in the [console](https://console.autotournament.gg). It never expires, has no server limit and enforces nothing. Want it in writing? [Tell us about your event](mailto:sivert@autotournament.gg?subject=Free%20LAN%20confirmation%3A%20%3Cevent%3E) (name, dates, website, organizer, roughly how many servers, how entry fees and sponsor money are used, and whether anyone is paid) and we'll send you a free confirmation. It's optional: the license already allows non-commercial use.

## Commercial use needs a license

Examples of commercial use:

- Hosting or running Auto Tournament for someone else for money, including managed hosting and "tournaments as a service".
- Selling Auto Tournament, a modified version of it, or a product or service built on it.
- Running tournaments or events where the organizer makes a profit.
- Using it inside a business, for example an esports organization, venue or company running events.
- Being paid to set up, install or operate Auto Tournament, CS2 Server Manager or Ready Up for someone else, even for a flat fee, and even when that event is itself free.

Forks and modified versions stay under the same license; they can't be relicensed (for example as MIT) or used commercially without a license.

Not sure whether your use counts as commercial? Ask first.

A paid-up license never stops anything. An unpaid monthly or yearly license keeps working for 14 days after the last day you paid for, then the software stops until it's paid. A key also stops on a second Auto Tournament install, and an old key stops a day after you make a new one. Your data is never deleted. Founding supporter licenses never stop. ## What a licensed install sends

Without a key, the platform sends nothing to Auto Tournament. With a key saved (a free key too), it checks in with autotournament.gg once a day: shortly after startup, when the key changes, and then daily. Before creating game servers with a paid key, it also asks whether they fit the license. It sends:

- the license key, so its id and signature can be checked (only the id is stored)
- a random id for this installation, made once, and its public address if it has one, so one key stays on one install
- the number of game servers
- the platform version
- counts since the last check-in: finished matches, tournaments with activity, and the largest team count
- for an event license, your answer to "Planning something new?" (testing, a new event, or moved dates), if you gave one

It sends no names, players or match details. The answer brings the license's current terms (servers, paid until) and the servers other hosts use on the same license. If a check-in fails (offline, a LAN without internet), nothing happens; creating servers with a paid key then relies on the last check-in, as long as it's at most 3 days old. You can see the last check-in under **Settings > License**. How long we keep the data is in the [Privacy Policy](https://autotournament.gg/privacy#license-checkin).

CS2 Server Manager hosts with a paid key check in the same way, with their server count.

For an event license that is used on days outside its dates, the admin dashboard may show a small note asking what's going on. Testing and setting up need no action. It shows at most once every 30 days, and you can turn it off for that license.

## Unlicensed commercial use

If we notify you in writing that your use is commercial and unlicensed, PolyForm gives you 32 days (first notice only) to come into compliance: stop the commercial use or buy a license, and put right past use. To settle past use, we offer a back-dated license at the normal price plus 50%. If you don't come into compliance within 32 days, all your PolyForm licenses end and we may claim compensation under the Norwegian Copyright Act (åndsverkloven § 81).

- **MIT:** the CS2 plugin [MatchZy Enhanced](https://github.com/Auto-Tournament/cs2-plugin) (now named Auto Tournament CS2), free for any use, including paid work. It is forked from [MatchZy](https://github.com/shobhit-pathak/MatchZy), which is MIT, and built on CounterStrikeSharp: GPLv3, with an exception letting plugins use MIT; a noncommercial license isn't allowed. Ready Up is a separate, native plugin, not a fork of MatchZy, and it is not MIT.
