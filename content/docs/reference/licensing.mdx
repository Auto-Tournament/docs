Auto Tournament is licensed under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://github.com/Auto-Tournament/auto-tournament/blob/main/LICENSE). The source is public and free to use, change and share for non-commercial purposes. It is not "open source" in the OSI sense.

## Free, no permission needed

- Personal use, hobby projects, learning and research.
- Running tournaments and LAN events that are free to enter.
- Use by non-profit organizations: charities, schools and universities, public research, health and safety organizations, and government bodies, as the license allows.
- Changing the code, forking it and sharing your changes, as long as you keep the LICENSE and the `Required Notice` line, and your use stays non-commercial.

## Commercial use needs a license from the author

Examples of commercial use:

- Hosting or running Auto Tournament for someone else for money, including managed hosting and "tournaments as a service".
- Selling Auto Tournament, a modified version of it, or a product or service built on it.
- Using it to run tournaments or events that charge players, teams or visitors to take part or attend (entry fees, tickets), unless you are a non-profit organization as listed above.
- Using it inside a business, for example an esports organization, venue or company running events for profit.
- Offering paid setup, installation or operation of Auto Tournament, CS2 Server Manager or Ready Up for clients, even for a flat fee.

Forks and modified versions stay under the same license; they can't be relicensed (for example as MIT) or used commercially without a license.

Not sure whether your use counts as commercial? Ask first.

## Getting a commercial license

Email the author, Sivert Gullberg Hansen, at [sivert@autotournament.gg](mailto:sivert@autotournament.gg). Include your name or company, country, VAT ID if you have one, the event date(s) or yearly start date, how many server seats you need and which option. You get an invoice, and once it's paid, a written confirmation naming the licensee, option, seats and period. Buy it before the event. Not sure what you need? Ask; the answer is free.

You pay per **server seat**: every game server you set up for the event, spares included. Prices in EUR, excluding VAT:

| Option | Per event (up to 5 days) | Yearly (unlimited events) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plugin only: Auto Tournament CS2 (formerly MatchZy Enhanced), MIT | Free | Free |
| Servers: CS2 Server Manager and/or the Ready Up plugin | €3 per seat | €12 per seat |
| Platform + servers: the full Auto Tournament setup | €5 per seat | €20 per seat |
| Hosting or resale (Auto Tournament as a service) | Custom quote | Custom quote |

Non-profit and community events, where entry only covers costs, get 50% off. Example: 32 servers plus 2 spares for one event is 34 seats, so €102 for servers only or €170 with the platform.

This is a first version of the pricing. If it doesn't fit your case, email and we'll work it out. More worked examples are on [Licensing & pricing](https://autotournament.gg/pricing).

## Which license covers what

- **PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0:** the platform ([auto-tournament](https://github.com/Auto-Tournament/auto-tournament)), the [CS2 Server Manager](https://github.com/Auto-Tournament/cs2-server-manager), the [Ready Up plugin](https://github.com/Auto-Tournament/ready-up), the [game packs](https://github.com/Auto-Tournament/packs), this documentation and the website. Packs published before 2026-09-24 were MIT and stay MIT.
- **MIT:** the [Auto Tournament CS2 plugin](https://github.com/Auto-Tournament/cs2-plugin) (formerly MatchZy Enhanced), free for any use, including paid work. It is forked from MatchZy, which is MIT, and it is built on CounterStrikeSharp, whose license lets plugins use MIT but not a noncommercial license.

## Older versions

Versions up to and including 2.4.15 (released as "MatchZy Auto Tournament") were published under the MIT License and remain under it. Everything since the rename to Auto Tournament is under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.
